# Measuring

## At the green, with the flag

1. Cut the hole and set the flag, as always.
2. Clip the marker on just under the flag. The flagstick centres it over the
   cup, and the case keeps the antenna within a few centimetres of the stick.
3. Hold the flag straight and press the button. The bubble shows when it
   stands upright. A few seconds later the phone buzzes once: saved. Three
   buzzes: no position yet; wait a moment and press again.
4. Take the marker off and walk on. The phone keeps count of the holes done
   and still to do. Back at the shed, the pins go out.

Switch the marker on in the shed. The receiver needs five to ten minutes to
settle and stays on for the round.

## On the round, with a pole

A course whose club records no pins can still get them. Screw the marker
onto a pole of about a metre with a flag ferrule at the bottom; it fits in
the bag, carried with the antenna up. At each green, set the pole in the
cup, hold it straight and press the button.

After a stretch under trees the receiver needs a few minutes to settle
again; until then it buzzes three times. Ferrules differ by cup maker, so
the pole takes the one that fits the course.

These pins go to OpenPinsheet through a channel of their own, apart from the
club's.
